Site Plan for Stellar Travel

Site Name

Stellar Travel

This name was chosen to immediately communicate the site's focus on **space tourism and interstellar journeys**. "Stellar" evokes the excitement and scale of celestial bodies, while "Travel" clearly indicates the service provided: high-end, futuristic travel planning.

Optional domain availability: stellartravel.com

Site Purpose

The site serves as a premium guide platform and information hub for future space study, research and tourism. It provides detailed travel packages to various celestial destinations (Moon, Mars, Orbital Stations), comprehensive safety and preparedness information.

Scenarios

  • A user wants to research about celestial bodies such as moons, stars, planets, nebulae and galaxies. How does the site facilitate this research?
  • A first-time space traveler is concerned about gravity sickness and G-forces. Where on the site can they find detailed, easy-to-understand documentation about pre-flight training and required medical certifications? They can also find information regarding satelite guide and studies

Color Schema

Deep Space (#0a1930): Primary color, used for section backgrounds and main text on light areas, suggesting a dark cosmic theme.

Star White (#f4f4f9): Light color, used for the main page background and text within dark sections, ensuring high contrast.

Cyber Teal (#00bcd4): Secondary color, used for key headings and subtle accents, representing technology and data streams.

Nebula Orange (#ff9800): Accent color, used for call-to-action elements, critical highlights, and demarcation lines.

Typography

Heading Font: 'Space Mono', monospace: Used for headings and code-like elements to provide a clean, technological, and futuristic interface.

Body Font: 'Roboto', sans-serif: Used for body text and long descriptions for modern readability and clarity.
